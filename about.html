ABOUT QUICK BITE

Making Campus Food Simple & Convenient

Quick Bite is a college canteen food ordering and delivery system designed especially for teachers and staff.

🍱

🍴 Who We Are

Quick Bite is a field survey project developed to make college canteen food ordering easier and faster. The system allows teachers and staff to browse the canteen menu, select food items, add them to a cart, provide delivery details and place an order.

The project also provides order tracking, location verification and feedback facilities.

🎯 Our Objectives

🍽️

Easy Ordering

Make food ordering simple and convenient for teachers and staff.

⚡

Save Time

Reduce waiting time at the college canteen.

📍

Location Check

Verify that the delivery location is within the permitted 1 KM area.

🚚

Order Tracking

Allow users to check the current status of their food order.
